About my duck

Once upon a time, a tiny duck named Pip lived in a peaceful, sunlit pond. Pip was incredibly cute, with fluffy yellow feathers, big round eyes, and a bright orange beak that always looked like it was smiling.Every morning, Pip would wake up, stretch his little wings, and do a happy dance on a lily pad. One chilly autumn day, he found a bright red knitted scarf dropped by a child near the pond. Pip carefully wrapped the long scarf around his neck, though it was so big it trailed behind him like a royal cape.As he waddled around the park, all the other animals couldn't help but gasp at how adorable he looked.
